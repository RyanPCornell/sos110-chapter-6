Population (P) is the total number of people on Earth. P scales I linearly — a 10% increase in P produces a 10% increase in I, holding A and T constant. Current global population is ~8.1 billion; UN projections estimate 9.7 billion by 2050.
A
GDP per Capita
$11,000
$50,000 / person
$1,000 / person
Affluence (A) measures per-capita GDP in thousands of dollars per year. Doubling A doubles I. The global average is ~$11,000/yr, ranging from under $1,000 in low-income nations to over $80,000 in high-income ones.
T
Carbon Intensity
0.39 kg/$
0.78 kg/$ (2× baseline)
0 kg/$ (no emissions)
Carbon Intensity (T) measures kg of CO₂ emitted per dollar of GDP. Lower T means cleaner energy and processes. At 0.39 kg/$, it reflects the 2020 global average. Halving T halves total emissions regardless of P and A.
